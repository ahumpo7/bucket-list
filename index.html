Google Cloud Sync Setup

Connect your free Firebase project so trips are saved to your Google account and sync live to your girlfriend's phone!

Easy 2-Minute Setup:

  1. Go to Firebase Console and click Create a project (free).
  2. Under Authentication > Sign-in method, enable Google.
  3. Under Firestore Database, click Create database (choose Test Mode).
  4. Under Project Settings (gear icon) > General > Your apps, click Web (</>) and paste your configuration keys below!
